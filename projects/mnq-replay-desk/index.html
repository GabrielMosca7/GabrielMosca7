Accepted formats

1-minute bars work best (higher timeframes are built from them). Unix timestamps are always read as UTC; the chart displays New York time.

TradingView export: time,open,high,low,close,Volume
NinjaTrader: 20261027 093100;21400.25;21405;21398.5;21403;812
Generic: Date,Time,Open,High,Low,Close,Volume

Simulation settings

Stops and targets trigger on 1-minute highs and lows. If a stop and target are both touched inside the same minute, the stop is assumed to fill first.

Keep visible in Focus Mode
The chart, drawing tools, timeframes and replay controls always stay. Press F to toggle.

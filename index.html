Maple & Bean Q3 Dashboard

A one-page sales review that tells a small café owner when to staff up, what sells, and which price to look at first.

The problem

A café owner had a quarter of sales and a cost sheet, but no quick way to see busy periods or which menu items earn the least per sale.

What I built

A Python script cleans the sales and sums them by month, week, weekday, hour and menu item, then joins the cost sheet to work out margins. The dashboard shows totals, trends and a margin table.

What it shows

  • Data cleaning and aggregation
  • KPI design: revenue, gross profit, orders, average order
  • Margin analysis that flags items under 70%

The takeaway

The page leads with one plain-language recommendation, built from the data, so the owner knows what to act on without reading every chart.

Replay Log

A journal for futures practice sessions that scores each one against prop-firm evaluation rules.

The problem

Practice sessions in TradingView's replay mode produce stats that are easy to lose and hard to compare from one week to the next.

What I built

An app that logs trades, win rate, profit factor, P&L and drawdown for each session, checks it against a profit target and loss limits, and charts the trend. It can also read the numbers from a screenshot of the stats panel.

What it shows

  • Metric design, like win rate weighted by trade count
  • Rule-based pass and fail flags
  • Filters by setup and tag, plus CSV export for Excel

Why it matters

It turns a pile of one-off results into a record that shows whether a strategy is actually improving.

Parlay Lab

A probability tool that checks whether a multi-leg bet has positive expected value, and how much to risk if it does.

The problem

Listed odds include the bookmaker's margin, so the real probability behind a price is hidden, and combining several legs hides it further.

What I built

A calculator that removes the margin to find a fair probability for each leg, compares it with the user's own estimate, and combines legs into expected value per $100.

What it shows

  • Probability and expected-value modelling
  • Risk sizing with a quarter-Kelly stake
  • Flags when an assumption breaks, such as two legs from one game

Why it matters

The same thinking applies to any decision under uncertainty: compare your estimate with the market's, size the risk, and track whether you were right.
